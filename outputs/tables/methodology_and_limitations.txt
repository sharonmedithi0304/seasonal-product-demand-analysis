SEASONAL PRODUCT DEMAND ANALYSIS

Primary comparison: January-November 2010 versus January-November 2011.

The candidate screening rules require at least 10 distinct invoices in each year, the same peak month in both years, a peak-to-average monthly quantity ratio of at least 1.5 in each year, and at least 10 distinct invoices during each peak month.

The analysis identified 292 recurring-peak candidates from 2,358 eligible products. November was the most frequent peak month, with 156 candidates. These figures are expected results and must pass the validation checks above.

These are exploratory recurring-peak candidates, not statistically proven long-term seasonal products. The data contains only two comparable annual windows.

The primary analysis excludes non-positive quantities and cancellation-coded invoices, so it describes retained positive-quantity transactions rather than net sales after returns. Zero-price rows were retained and counted. Missing customer identifiers were allowed because customer-level identification is not required for product-level analysis.
